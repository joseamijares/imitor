The hosted service is being built in the open. Nothing here is a promise of a date or a price.

What will it cost?
Not decided. The plan is to charge for running it (storage, longer history, seats and support), not to lock features away: the server you can host yourself has all of them. The waitlist hears the price first.
When does it open?
No date. It works today for a small invite-only group at app.imitor.dev, and it stays that way until it is ready to be relied on.
Do I need it to use Imitor?
No. Everything under Open source runs on your machine, in CI or in your agent’s session, and imitor export publishes a run as a static site anywhere.
What happens to my address?
It is kept for the waitlist and used once, to say the hosted service is open. Write to {{contact}} to be taken off it.
